The Neighborhood Development Health Report (NDHR) compiles existing Health Department data related to urban planning and development. Indicators are grouped into categories reflecting different social determinants of health, reported at the community district geography, and compared with borough and citywide values.

## NYC Healthy Places Toolkit

A holistic consideration of the impacts of urban planning on public health must include an understanding of existing community health conditions. Various factors – including inequitable distribution of wealth, harmful past policies such as redlining, and historical and current concentration of polluting actions – have contributed to health disparities, resulting in some communities with resident life expectancy significantly shorter than other communities based on zip code. These disparities in health outcomes can be worsened by additional exposures.

The Healthy Places Toolkit provides a baseline snapshot, equipping City Agencies, Community Based Organizations, and Private Developers with the necessary data to understand community conditions as new developments or planning processes happening across the city. This toolkit was developed by the Health Equity in Urban Planning workgroup within the NYC Health Department.


## Health Equity in Urban Planning

The Health Equity in Urban Planning (HEiUP) workgroup is a cross-divisional team of health strategists at the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene promoting health equity in urban planning processes. We are improving health outcomes through building towards a society that supports health and well-being while confronting the past. We aim to create a healthy and thriving city for all New Yorkers through adopting vetted, realistic, and maximally effective solutions.
