Environmental health data can come at several different 'geographies' - neighborhood boundary schemes. Often, health data are available at a neighborhood boundary scheme called UHF42, which breaks up NYC into 42 neighborhoods.

If you're looking for data for a Community District or a City Council District, but the data are only available by UHF42 neighborhood, then use this page to get the UHF42s that overlap your Community District or Council District. 

Overlap is shown both by area and by population, and links will take you to our Neighborhood Reports.
