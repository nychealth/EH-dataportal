We collect data using two different types of air quality monitors:

<strong>Integrated monitors</strong> are the gold standard monitors for measuring air quality in ways that are relevant to public health. They are placed in sites designed to reflect long-term neighborhood-level exposures to pollution. Integrated monitors measure 5 different pollutants:
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5), a major pollutant that harms health
- Nitrogen dioxide (NO2), the best indicator of traffic-related air pollution
- Nitric oxide (NO)
- Ozone (O3)
- Black carbon (BC)

<strong>Realtime monitors</strong> are placed next to high-traffic roadways. They are best at capturing daily PM2.5 patterns and correlating them with traffic levels.
