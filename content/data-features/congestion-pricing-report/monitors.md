We collect data using two different types of air quality monitors:

<strong><i class="fas fa-smog mr-1" aria-hidden=true></i>Integrated monitors</strong> are the gold standard monitors for measuring air quality in ways that are relevant to public health. They are placed in sites designed to reflect long-term neighborhood-level exposures to pollution. Integrated monitors measure 5 different pollutants:
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5), a major pollutant that harms health
- Nitrogen dioxide (NO2), the best indicator of traffic-related air pollution
- Nitric oxide (NO)
- Ozone (O3)
- Black carbon (BC)

<strong><i class="fas fa-clock mr-1" aria-hidden="true"></i>Realtime monitors</strong> are placed next to high-traffic roadways and measure PM2.5. They are best at capturing daily patterns, so that we can correlate them with traffic levels.
