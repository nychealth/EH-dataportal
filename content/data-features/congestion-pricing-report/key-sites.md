##### Air quality monitoring and analysis for CRZ tolling includes five pollutants:

- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5)
- Nitrogen dioxide (NO2)
- Nitric oxide (NO)
- Ozone (O3)
- Black carbon (BC)

NO2 is the best indicator of traffic-related air pollution. The NYCCAS network deploys monitors at 84 sites across the city once per season. At a subset of these sites, located in EJ-designated communities identified in the Environmental Assessment, the Health Department increased the frequency of monitoring and deployed additional real time PM2.5 and BC monitors.
