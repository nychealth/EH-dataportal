<h3 class="h4"> Pollution outside of NYC </h3>

At EPA monitoring sites, average PM2.5 and NO2 levels from July to September 2025 were in the higher end of the range measured at the same sites July-September 2022-2024.  

The higher levels at sites farther away from the CRZ in Connecticut and Eastern Long Island, suggest that regional pollution levels remained similar or even increased slightly in 2025.  

Using a control site for comparison at sites within NYC helps to take into account these larger scale trends in the evaluation of the air quality impact of the CRZ tolling program.
