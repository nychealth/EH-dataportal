### Pollution outside of the CRZ and NYC

Outside of the CRZ, pollution levels did not change.​

Average pollution levels in the region outside of NYC also did not change. At EPA monitoring sites on the map below, PM2.5, NO2 and O3 levels following the start of CRZ tolling were similar to levels prior to tolling.
