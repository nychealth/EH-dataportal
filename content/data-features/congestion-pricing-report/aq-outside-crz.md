<h3 class="h4"> Pollution outside of the CRZ and NYC </h3>
Pollution outside of NYC.​

At EPA monitoring sites on the map below, average PM2.5 and NO2 levels from July – September 2025 fell in the higher end of the range measured at the same sites July-September 2022-2024.  The higher levels at sites farther away from the CRZ in Connecticut and Eastern Long Island, suggest that regional pollution levels remained similar or even increased slightly in 2025.  Using a control site for comparison at sites within NYC helps to take into account these larger scale trends in the evaluation of the air quality impact of the CRZ tolling program.
