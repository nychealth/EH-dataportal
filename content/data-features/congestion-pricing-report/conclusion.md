This evaluation focused on the impact of the CRZ tolling program on air pollution levels in neighborhoods at locations that have been monitored for years through the New York City Community Air Survey.  The additional monitoring supported by the MTA allowed us to determine whether levels of PM2.5, NO2, O3 and BC were different than they would have been if tolling had never been started.  We found that at EJ neighborhood sites, Congestion Relief Zone tolling did not cause air pollution to be any different than it would have been without tolling. We also found that within the CRZ, neighborhood pollution levels were no different with tolling than if tolling had never started.  These findings, along with the improvements to the transit system and mitigation measures, emphasize the benefits that the tolling program is bringing to New York City as a whole.
