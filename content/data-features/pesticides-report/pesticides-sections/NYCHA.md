The New York City Housing Authority (NYCHA) manages more than 2,200 residential buildings and more than 170,000 apartments, as well as office space and grounds that cover approximately 2,300 acres. NYCHA currently has a staff of more than 100 pest management professionals who perform routine inspections and treatments, carry out specialized intensive integrated pest management interventions, apply pesticides, and respond to complaints and requests from other agencies. NYCHA staff members regularly address a range of insect and rodent problems, including interior rodents, cockroaches and bed bugs, and exterior rats, and weeds.
