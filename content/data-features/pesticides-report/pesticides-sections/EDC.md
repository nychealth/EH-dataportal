The New York City Economic Development Corporation (NYCEDC) fosters inclusive economic development across all five boroughs by helping to lay the foundations of growth, advancing quality jobs across sectors and promoting access to opportunity. NYCEDC is responsible for contracting for pest control, including rodent control, in publicly accessible open spaces within the lots of private buildings.
