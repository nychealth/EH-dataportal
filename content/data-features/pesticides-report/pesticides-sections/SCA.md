The School Construction Authority (SCA) designs and constructs safe, attractive and environmentally sound public schools for children throughout the many communities of New York City. They are dedicated to building and modernizing schools in a responsible, cost-effective manner while achieving the highest standards of excellence in safety, quality, and integrity.
