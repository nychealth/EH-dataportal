NYC Public Schools (NYCPS) provides primary and secondary education to more than 1 million pre-kindergarten to grade 12 students. Pest management at all New York City public schools is overseen by their Pest Control Unit. There are approximately 20 in-house licensed pest management professionals who conduct monthly monitoring inspections and respond to pest problems for approximately 900 schools. NYCPS contracts with several pest management companies to provide services for an additional 300 schools. NYS Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) more strictly regulates pesticide use at schools. More information is available here: https://www.dec.ny.gov/chemical/41822.html.
