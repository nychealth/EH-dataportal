The Department of Sanitation (DSNY) manages garages, office buildings, transfer stations, and various publicly owned lots throughout the city. Because of the nature of the agency's work – managing garbage – pest problems are endemic at some facilities, so DSNY employs two certified pesticide applicators who make routine visits to facilities and respond to emergency calls.
