The City University of New York (CUNY) is the nation's largest urban public university and consists of 25 institutions. The University serves more than 225,000 degree-credit students and 273,000 adult continuing and professional education students. Individual institutions contract for pest control services through private vendors. The data reports were received from a subset of the following 11 colleges: Borough of Manhattan Community College, Brooklyn College, College of Staten Island, NYC College of Technology, The Graduate Center, Hunter College, John Jay College, Kingsborough Community College, LaGuardia Community College, Queens College, and Queens Borough Community College.
