The main finding is simple: on hotter days, there is a greater risk of being injured or becoming ill at work. In NYC, this pattern is seen across nearly all job sectors, also known as industries, not just those traditionally associated with heat exposure. However, the increase in risk is greatest in industries where outdoor or indoor heat exposure is a regular part of the job, such as construction, delivery, and food service. For example, on a hot 95°F day, workers in these industries were injured 14% more often than on a 70°F day, roughly three times the increase seen across all other jobs.
