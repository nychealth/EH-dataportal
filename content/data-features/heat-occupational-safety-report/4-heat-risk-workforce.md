Heat risk at work is not limited to agriculture, construction, or other outdoor jobs. Across most NYC industries, workplace injury and illness risk was already elevated on 80°F days compared to 70°F days, with larger increases generally observed at higher temperatures (Figure 4).

<div class="asidebox">Results are based on 372,334 workers’ compensation claims in NYC from May through September, 2016–2025.</div>

The workers affected reflect NYC’s diverse workforce (Figure 5), including construction workers, taxi drivers, couriers, warehouse workers, janitors, restaurant cooks, and correctional officers. For most occupations, risk begins to rise more sharply in the mid-70s and continues increasing as temperatures climb.
