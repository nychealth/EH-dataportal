We used our estimates of how workplace injury and illness risk changes with temperature to calculate the number of heat-related injuries and illnesses occurring across different temperature ranges.

Most heat-related injuries occur before temperatures reach levels considered “extremely hot”. We estimate that about 1,415 reported heat-related workplace injuries and illnesses occur each warm season in NYC -- about 9 injuries or illnesses entering the workers’ compensation system each day from May through September.

Although the risk of injury is greatest on the hottest days, days with a temperature at or above 95°F were relatively rare during the study period. Moderately hot days occur much more often, so 98% of heat-related injuries occur on days below 95°F. As heat waves become more frequent in NYC, however, the burden associated with those temperatures may also increase.
