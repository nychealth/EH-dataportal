New York City has approximately 30,000 restaurants that the Health Department inspects every year to monitor compliance with city and state food safety regulations. At the end of an inspection, the Health Department gives restaurants a score, which is eventually converted to a grade of A, B, or C. This site describes the distribution of those letter grades across New York City restaurants.

### Restaurant Food Safety and Health

Safe food handling can protect diners from foodborne illness. During inspections, health inspectors look to ensure that restaurants and their employees are maintaining proper food safety practices. They measure food temperature; look for evidence of food contamination; check for signs of roaches and other vermin; and examine a restaurant’s cooking, refrigeration, and cleaning equipment. If an inspector finds violations, they mark points on their inspection checklist. More points mean a lower grade. If conditions are so bad that they pose a public health hazard, the Health Department may close the restaurant until they are fixed.

### About the Data and Indicators

The NYC Health Department began issuing grades and requiring restaurants to post them in July 2010. This portal presents the number and percent of restaurants operating with an A, B, or C in New York City.

For 2020, the numbers provided were as of March 16, 2020; whereas the data provided are as of July 1 for every other year. Additionally, data from 2021 and 2022 were excluded, due to reduced food inspection activity during the COVID-19 pandemic, as the NYC Department of Health shifted its priorities to address pandemic-related needs during this period.

### Restaurants with Letter Grades (A, B, or C)

An A grade is the highest a restaurant can score in a health inspection. It indicates a well-maintained restaurant with clean and safe food handling and storage practices, minimizing the risk of food poisoning. B and C grades indicate that a restaurant has violations that need improvement, with C being the lowest grade and requiring the most corrective action. A "Grade Pending" status means the restaurant is waiting for a final grade after addressing violations.
