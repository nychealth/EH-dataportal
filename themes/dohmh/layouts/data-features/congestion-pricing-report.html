Air quality changes throughout the day

We used realtime monitors to measure PM2.5 levels throughout the day, capturing daily patterns that filter-based integrated monitors can't. These realtime monitors measure PM2.5 levels every hour, giving us a detailed picture of how pollution changes throughout the day. These data let us examine correlations with traffic volumes. Notice that the time of day when the traffic is highest is not always the time of day that PM2.5 is highest. This is because other sources of PM2.5, like building heating and restaurant cooking, contribute to the levels of PM2.5 in the air.
