{{- with .Resources.GetMatch "analysis.md" -}} {{ .Content }} {{- end -}}

The vertical bars show the Standard Error. Think of them as a reality check on the data: a shorter bar means we collected more data, and we are more certain that our calculated average isn't a fluke or coincidence.

Second chart

This shows the difference between the observed and hypothetical values, after congestion pricing. The bar is the 95% confidence interval. If the bar crosses 0, it means that the difference between Observed and Hypothetical is not statistically significant: congestion pricing didn't cause an observable change in pollution levels.
