Indicator ranking

Each indicator is placed in one of three categories. that shows how the neighborhood compares to other NYC neighborhoods, if you ranked all of the NYC neighborhoods based on this indicator.

Better
In the top third of all NYC neighorhoods.

Middle
Close to the NYC median - ranking in the middle third of all neighborhoods.

Worse
In the bottom third of NYC neighborhoods

These are only based on the distribution of data values, not the range. The absolute values of indicators in different categories may not be all that different.
