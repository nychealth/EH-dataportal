How did traffic and PM2.5 near the highways change?

With hourly traffic and PM2.5 data, we can look at the time of day that levels measured alongside the highways were highest before and after CRZ tolling began. We see that traffic was highest during morning or evening rush hours and that PM2.5 levels were also highest during rush hours but also overnight. This is due to the influence of other pollution sources like heating systems in buildings, restaurant cooking and construction.​
